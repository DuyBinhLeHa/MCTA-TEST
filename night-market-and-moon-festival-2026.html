INTERNATIONAL NIGHT MARKET & MOON FESTIVAL 2026

🌕✨ Discover a World of Culture, Cuisine, and Celebration at the International Night Market & Moon Festival 2026! 🌏🏮

Join us for two unforgettable evenings where cultures from around the globe come together in the heart of Morrow. Experience an exciting blend of authentic international street food, live cultural performances, artisan vendors, family-friendly activities, and the enchanting traditions of the Mid-Autumn Festival.

📅 Date: Saturday & Sunday, October 3 & 4, 2026

📍 Location: The District – 1065 Olde Towne Morrow Rd, Morrow, GA 30260

⏰ Time: 6:00 PM – 12:00 AM

International Culture, Music & Street Food

Embark on a cultural journey through vibrant performances, traditional dances, live music, and captivating entertainment representing communities from around the world. Wander through our bustling international marketplace featuring handcrafted goods, unique cultural exhibits, and an incredible selection of authentic street food from Asia, Europe, Africa, Latin America, and beyond.

Whether you're discovering new flavors, shopping for handcrafted treasures, or enjoying performances from talented local and international artists, every corner of the festival offers something exciting for visitors of all ages.

Moon Festival Traditions & Family Fun

As evening falls, celebrate the timeless traditions of the Moon Festival with glowing lantern displays, cultural storytelling, and joyful moments shared with family and friends. Experience the beauty of one of Asia's most cherished celebrations while embracing the diversity that makes our community so special.

Bring your loved ones, wear your traditional attire, enjoy the festive atmosphere, and create lasting memories under the autumn moon. From spectacular entertainment and delicious cuisine to cultural experiences and community spirit, the International Night Market & Moon Festival 2026 is a celebration you won't want to miss.
